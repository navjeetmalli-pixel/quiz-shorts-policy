Quiz Shorts Uploader is a personal, single-user application that publishes original educational quiz videos (geography, flags, capitals, science, history, general knowledge and more) to the owner's own YouTube channel using YouTube API Services. It is not distributed to, or usable by, anyone other than the channel owner.

▶ Watch the quizzes on YouTube

What it does

  • Uploads finished quiz videos to the owner's channel (videos.insert)
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags and thumbnail
  • Creates category playlists and adds each video to them
  • Reads back the owner's own channel, video and playlist details to confirm success

What it does not do

  • It never accesses any other user's YouTube data
  • It stores no viewer or subscriber data
  • It does not embed, play or modify the YouTube player
